# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique work rather than people. Welcome differing experience and accessibility needs. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, post sexualized material directed at participants, expose private information, or continue unwanted contact.

This applies in repository issues, reviews, discussions and other spaces maintained for the project. Maintainers may edit/remove content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation or ban repeated/severe misconduct. Actions should be proportionate and include an explanation when possible. Maintainers follow the same rules.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's private reporting channel described in SECURITY.md, or use GitHub's reporting tools. Include the relevant context without reposting private information publicly. Reports will be handled discreetly; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
